ITIN Application

Complete this intake so Filings4u can review the information needed for a Form W-7 ITIN application or renewal.

An ITIN is for individuals who need a U.S. taxpayer identification number for federal tax purposes but are not eligible for an SSN. Most W-7 applications require a federal tax return unless an IRS exception applies.
